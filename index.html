    About Us

    Why Reel Steel

    Reel Steel Constructions LLC builds metal carports, steel buildings, shops, garages and sheds for customers in the Pacific Northwest, including Klamath Falls, Oregon. We work with you from the first idea to the final bolt.

    • Custom to your site. Sizes, heights, doors, windows and colors chosen around how you will actually use the space.
    • Built for Northwest weather. Options like vertical roofing and heavier 12-gauge framing to help handle snow and rain.
    • Straightforward quotes. We visit the site, talk through options, and give you a clear written quote before work starts.
    • One crew, start to finish. Framing, sheeting, trim and doors installed by the same team.
